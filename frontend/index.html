FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common questions about lab booking and permits.

How long does Lab Technician approval take after submitting?

Requests submitted during service hours (Mon – Sat, 07:30 – 17:00 WIB) are typically reviewed within 15 to 45 minutes. Once approved, your QR E-Ticket and notification appear automatically.

Can I edit or cancel a reservation if my team schedule changes?

Yes. Open My Bookings while your request is still Pending to edit the room, time slot, or participant count—or cancel anytime so the slot opens back up for other students.

Do all team members need to book separately for one project session?

No. Only one person in charge (PIC) needs to submit the booking, then list the total participants and team members inside the form.

Why is a specific room marked as Maintenance?

Labs under hardware upgrades, OS imaging, or exam setup are temporarily locked by Lab Staff. Use Smart Slot Finder to immediately locate another operational room with matching software.
